Think of AI like a big family.

```

Artificial Intelligence (AI)

↓

Machine Learning (ML)

↓

Deep Learning (DL)

↓

Neural Networks
```

### Artificial Intelligence (AI)

AI means making computers act smart.

Examples

- Siri
- Alexa
- ChatGPT
- Self-driving cars

Think of AI as teaching computers to solve problems.

---

Instead of writing thousands of rules...

we let the computer learn from examples.

### Deep Learning

Deep Learning is a special kind of Machine Learning.

It uses many layers of "brain-like" neurons.

It is used for

- recognizing faces
- translating languages
- playing games
- creating images

# Chapter 2 - Meet the Artificial Brain

### Neural Network

A Neural Network is a team of tiny workers.

Each worker does one small job.

Together,

they solve difficult problems.

Think of it like students solving a big puzzle together.

---

### Neuron

A neuron is one tiny decision maker.

It receives information.

It thinks.

Then it sends the answer to the next neuron.

---

### Layer

Neurons are organized into layers.

```

Input Layer

↓

Hidden Layer

↓

Hidden Layer

↓

Output Layer

```

More layers

=

Deeper learning.

---

# Chapter 3 - Learning New Things

### Training

Training means teaching the computer.

Just like practicing multiplication.

The more good examples,

the smarter it becomes.

---

### Dataset

A dataset is the collection of examples.

Example

10,000 dog pictures

5,000 cat pictures

The computer learns from them.

---

### Epoch

One epoch means

the computer has looked at every picture once.

10 epochs

means

it has studied everything 10 times.

---

### Batch

Instead of reading 10,000 pictures at once,

the computer studies

100 at a time.

Each small group is called a batch.

---

# Chapter 4 - How the Computer Learns

### Prediction

The computer makes a guess.

Example

"I think this is a dog."

---

### Loss

Loss measures

How wrong was my guess?

Small loss

😊 Good

Large loss

😢 Needs improvement

---

### Backpropagation

The computer looks at its mistakes,

learns from them,

and becomes better.

Exactly like correcting homework.

---

### Gradient Descent

Imagine walking down a mountain.

Every step gets you closer to the bottom.

Gradient Descent helps the computer find the best answer.

---

### Learning Rate

Should we take

tiny steps?

or

giant steps?

Learning Rate decides.

---

# Chapter 5 - Looking at Pictures

### CNN

CNN stands for

Convolutional Neural Network.

It is very good at understanding pictures.

It first finds

edges

↓

shapes

↓

objects

↓

the final answer.

---

### Filter

A filter is like wearing special glasses.

One filter sees edges.

Another sees circles.

Another sees colors.

---

### Pooling

Pooling keeps only the important information.

Imagine shrinking a huge picture into a smaller one

without losing the important parts.

---

# Chapter 6 - Reading and Speaking

### RNN

RNN remembers previous words.

Example

"I love ____"

It remembers

"I"

and

"love"

before predicting the next word.

---

### LSTM

LSTM is an improved RNN.

It has a much better memory.

It remembers important information longer.

---

### Attention

Attention means

"Pay attention to the important words."

Exactly like your teacher says.

---

### Transformer

Transformers use Attention.

They understand whole sentences at once.

ChatGPT is based on Transformer technology.

---

# Chapter 7 - Becoming Smarter

### Transfer Learning

Imagine

your older sister already knows algebra.

Learning geometry becomes easier.

Computers do the same thing.

They reuse what they already learned.

---

### Fine-Tuning

Fine-tuning means

giving extra lessons

to an already smart computer.

---

### Data Augmentation

Need more pictures?

Rotate them.

Flip them.

Zoom them.

Now one picture becomes many.

---

### Overfitting

The computer memorizes the homework

instead of understanding it.

Then

it fails the exam.

---

### Underfitting

The computer didn't learn enough.

It performs poorly everywhere.

---

### Regularization

Regularization helps the computer

avoid memorizing.

It encourages understanding instead.

---
